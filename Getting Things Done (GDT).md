Getting Things Done (GTD) is a personal productivity methodology developed by David Allen. It aims to help individuals organize their tasks, projects, and priorities to achieve a stress-free and efficient work life. The core idea is to capture all open loops, tasks, and projects, and then break them down into smaller, actionable steps.

**Key Components:**

- **Collect:** Write down all tasks, ideas, and projects that come to mind, including “loose ends” and “next actions.”
- **Clarify:** Define the next physical action for each task or project, making it clear what needs to be done.
- **Organize:** Categorize and prioritize tasks based on context, such as projects, areas of focus, or specific locations.
- **Reflect:** Regularly review and update your system to ensure it remains relevant and effective.
- **Engage:** Take action on the next step for each task or project, and delegate or defer as needed.

**GTD Tools:**

- **Inbox:** A centralized location for capturing new tasks and ideas.
- **Next Actions:** A list of the next physical steps for each task or project.
- **Contexts:** Categories for organizing tasks based on location, time, or energy required.
- **Waiting On:** A list of tasks that depend on others’ actions.
- **Someday/Maybe:** A list for ideas that are not currently actionable but may be in the future.
