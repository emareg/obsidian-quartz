Here I describe my experience with building a smart home using:

* KNX (mostly from MDT) for basic control: only needs power 
* Home Assistant for Automations: only needs LAN. 
	* KNX Integration for, well, writing/reading KNX commands
	* ZHA Integration for controlling Philips Hue lights 
* Alexa for voice commands: needs WAN (Internet)

In addition, I have the following devices
* A weather station on the roof: needs WLAN
* Philip Hue smart lights: need ZigBee dongle/hub
* Reolink Cameras: need PoE cables
