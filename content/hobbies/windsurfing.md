Some personal notes on windsurfing.
## Harness
* Put the straps first: Front foot, back foot
* Get into the harness: look forward
* Put 50% of your body weight on the harness lines: keep your center low (bend knees if low wind)
* Pull front arm to kill power

## Planning
* Steer with the mast! Small movements
* Pull front hand to release (kill) power
* Push with your heels, not with the thoes!


## Fast Tack
* Front foot in front of the mast
* Three arm steps and three foot steps: forward, cross, flip
* Extend your new front arm
* keep the rig away

## Jibe

* Body weight on the the tail
* Keep boom close to your chest (bent arms)
* There is a short moment with maximum sail surface


## Water-Start
* only back foot on board
* keep body center low
* hands together (shoulder width) and straight (max lift)
